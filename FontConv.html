Export & compatibility

Choose a target font format for individual downloads and ZIP exports. Metadata reports and CSS work independently of font binary export.

Supported export targets: TTF, OTF, WOFF and WOFF2, plus “keep original format” mode. Conversion uses a browser-loaded font engine and may require an internet connection. Complex collections (TTC/OTC), EOT, variable-font axis editing and some proprietary metadata may still require a dedicated font tool. Always test converted fonts in your target application.
